Support for DICE SHIFT

Contact

For questions, bugs or suggestions, write to bosselgo@web.de. Please mention your device, the version of the app and, for problems with a level, its number.

Frequently asked questions

How does rolling work?

Swipe in a direction and the die tips over an edge onto the next tile. A different side comes up, just like with a real die. Opposite sides always add up to 7. A level is solved when every target tile holds a die showing the matching number on top.

How do I get three stars?

The fewer moves you need, the more stars you get. The number next to the star in the top right shows how many moves earn three stars.

What do the special tiles do?

IceThe die slides on without turning until it hits an obstacle or the ice ends.
PortalThe die reappears on the partner portal if it is free.
SpringThe die jumps over the next tile without turning, even across gaps and blocks.
TurntableIt turns the die a quarter turn. The top number stays, the sides change.

How do I unlock the next world?

Solve 20 levels of the previous world. The world overview shows how many are still missing.

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap “Hint”. The next useful direction is free once per level. To see more moves ahead, pay with coins or choose to watch a short video.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress, stars, coins, designs and achievements are stored only on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. If you delete the app, your progress is gone.

I bought “Remove ads” and switched devices.

Open the settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the same Apple account you used for the purchase.

Why do I still see videos after the purchase?

The purchase removes the ads between levels. Videos for coins, hints or extra moves only start when you choose to watch them; they remain as an optional offer.

How do I change my ad choice?

In the app's settings, under “Privacy”, there is an entry for ad consent. There you can change or withdraw your consent at any time.
