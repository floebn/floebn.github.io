Summary

  • DICE SHIFT needs no account. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The provider runs no server of its own for the app and receives no data about you.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob. Data for advertising is only processed after you have been asked in the app. You can decline and still play without restrictions.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The provider never sees your payment details.

Data stored only on your device

The app stores locally on your device:

  • level progress (solved levels, stars, best number of moves)
  • coins, collected rewards and achievements
  • purchased and selected designs for dice and boards
  • settings (sounds, vibration, roll animation, confirm restart)
  • the status of the “Remove ads” purchase and how many optional videos you have watched today
  • anonymous game statistics: how often levels were started, solved and abandoned, and how many moves, hints and restarts there were. You can turn this off in the settings under “Anonymous game statistics”.

This data never leaves your device, including the statistics. It is deleted when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings it may be part of a device backup with Apple or Google; the provider has no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

There are two kinds of ads: optional videos you start yourself to receive coins, a hint or extra moves, and full-screen ads between two levels. Full-screen ads appear only after you have solved five levels, and never during a level.

Data Google may process:

  • device identifiers, in particular your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device information (model, operating system, language)
  • ad usage data (which ad was shown and whether it was tapped)
  • diagnostic data (crashes, performance data of the ad module)

Purposes: selecting, delivering and measuring ads, limiting how often they appear, preventing fraud.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks on first launch whether you consent to processing for advertising purposes. The form is provided by Google; you can accept, decline or choose individual purposes and vendors.

  • With consent, ads may be tailored to your interests.
  • Without consent, no personalised ads are shown. It is possible that no ads load at all. In that case the offers for optional videos may not be available; the game itself remains fully playable.

You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → ad consent. Withdrawal applies to the future.

Tracking request on iPhone

On Apple devices the operating system also asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the app gets no access to the device's advertising ID. You can change this later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android devices you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the system settings under Google → Ads.

In-app purchase “Remove ads”

The purchase is handled by the store you downloaded the app from: Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The store processes your account, the transaction data and your payment details. The provider receives no payment details. The app only stores that the purchase exists.

The purchase removes the full-screen ads between levels. You can still start optional videos yourself.

Store privacy policies: Apple, Google.

Recipients and transfers to third countries

Data recipients are Google (advertising) and Apple or Google as store operators. Data may be transferred to the USA and other countries outside the European Union. According to their own statements, Google and Apple base such transfers on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses.

More information: Google privacy policy, how Google processes data in its advertising products.

Legal bases

  • Advertising, where it accesses identifiers or information on your device: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG)
  • Processing the purchase: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Answering your emails: legitimate interest in communication (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Retention

Locally stored data remains until you delete the app. Google and the stores decide themselves how long they keep data; details are in their privacy policies. Support emails are deleted once your request is settled and no retention obligation applies.

Contact by email

If you write an email, your address and the content of your message are processed in order to reply to you.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. The DICE SHIFT pages set no cookies and load no content from other servers.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. As the provider stores no data about you, requests for access or deletion of advertising data are in practice directed to Google.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony.

Children

DICE SHIFT is not specifically directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app or the law changes. The version on this page applies.
