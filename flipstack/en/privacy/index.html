Privacy policy for FlipStack

The essentials

  • FlipStack does not need an account. Your progress is stored only on your device.
  • The provider does not run a server for the app and does not receive any data about you.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Data for advertising is only processed after you have been asked in the app. You can decline and still play without restrictions.
  • A purchase is handled by Apple or Google. The provider never sees your payment details.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

  • game statistics (high score, greatest height, rounds played, best combo)
  • coins, progress on daily and weekly quests, achievements
  • unlocked and selected designs
  • a round you have not finished yet, so that you can continue it
  • settings (sound, vibration, tutorial)
  • the status of the “Ad-Free” purchase and your decision about ads

This data does not leave your device. It is deleted when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings it may be part of a device backup with Apple or Google; the provider has no access to that.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob. The provider of this service is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

There are two kinds of ads: optional ads that you start yourself to receive a reward, and full-screen ads between two rounds. No ads appear during a round.

Data Google may process in this context:

  • device identifiers, in particular your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and, derived from it, your approximate location
  • information about the device (model, operating system, language)
  • usage data about ads (which ad was shown and whether it was tapped)
  • diagnostic data (crashes, performance data of the ad module)

Purposes: selecting, delivering and measuring ads, limiting how often they appear, and fraud prevention.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks on first launch whether you consent to processing for advertising purposes. The dialog is provided by Google; you can agree, decline or choose individual purposes and vendors.

  • With your consent, ads may be tailored to your interests.
  • Without your consent, no personalised ads are shown. It is possible that no ads are loaded at all. In that case the offers in exchange for optional ads may not be available; the game itself remains fully usable.

You can change your decision at any time in the app under Settings → “Privacy settings”. Withdrawal applies to the future.

Tracking prompt on iPhone and iPad

On Apple devices the operating system additionally asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the app has no access to the device's advertising ID. You can change this later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android devices you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the system settings under Google → Ads.

In-app purchase “Ad-Free”

The purchase is handled by the store you downloaded the app from: Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The store processes your account, the transaction data and your payment details. The provider does not receive any payment details. The app only stores the fact that the purchase exists.

Privacy information of the stores: Apple, Google.

Recipients and transfers to third countries

Recipients of data are Google (advertising) and Apple or Google as the operator of the store. Data may be transferred to the USA and other countries outside the European Union. According to their own statements, Google and Apple base such transfers on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses.

Further information: Google's privacy policy, Google's information on data processing in its advertising products.

Legal bases

  • Advertising, where it accesses identifiers or information on your device: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG)
  • Handling the purchase: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Answering your enquiries by email: legitimate interest in communication (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Storage period

Locally stored data remains until you delete the app. How long Google and the stores keep data is determined by them; details can be found in their privacy information. Emails to support are deleted once your request has been dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

Contact by email

If you write an email, your address and the content of your message are processed in order to reply to you.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. The FlipStack pages do not set cookies and do not load content from other servers.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because the provider does not store any data about you, requests for access to or deletion of advertising data are in practice directed at Google.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony, Germany.

Children

FlipStack is not specifically directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app or the legal situation changes. The version on this page applies.
