Support for FlipStack

Contact

For questions, bugs or suggestions, write to bosselgo@web.de. Please mention your device and the version of the app; that helps to reproduce the issue.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the round end?

The round ends when the current piece has no valid spot in any of the four views of the platform. A spot is valid if at least half of the piece is supported and it does not rise too far above the lowest square afterwards. The result screen shows you the piece and the reason.

What does the frame around the tower mean?

It shows the highest level you are currently allowed to build to. Fill the marked low squares first and the frame moves up.

Where is my progress stored?

High score, coins, quests and unlocked designs are stored only on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. If you delete the app, your progress is gone.

I bought “Ad-Free” and changed devices.

Open the settings in the app, tap “Ad-Free” and then “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the same Apple or Google account you used for the purchase.

How do I change my decision about ads?

The settings in the app contain an entry called “Privacy settings”. There you can change or withdraw your consent at any time.
